Norway, 7 days

Seven days on the Lofoten coast

Words and photos by Ingrid Dahl

The E10 runs the length of the islands, from Svolvær to the village of Å. We drove it in early September, after the summer crowds and before the first snow.

Most days start the same way: coffee on the quay in Henningsvær, then a short, steep climb before the light goes flat.

Bio 201, week 6

Cellular respiration

Cells release the energy stored in glucose in three stages. Most of the ATP comes from the last one, not from glycolysis.

The three stages

  • Glycolysis, in the cytoplasm: glucose is split into two pyruvate.
  • The citric acid cycle, in the mitochondrial matrix.
  • Oxidative phosphorylation, across the inner membrane.

For Thursday's quiz

Know where each stage happens, what goes in and what comes out. Redraw the electron transport chain from memory.

Ask for 1998.Get 1998.

A layout is one small file that says where everything goes and how it looks. This one trades the sidebar for a title bar and a taskbar.

layout: { arrangement: valerie-98 }  # config.yaml

Or make yourtabs sheep.

Click a sheep to go to its tab. The gate makes a new one. Plugins can redraw any part of the window: install one, or ask your AI to write it.

layout: { arrangement: pasture }

Miss Chrome?Keep its shape.

Tabs, toolbar and bookmarks where Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge keep them, with the shortcuts your hands already know.

layout: { arrangement: like-chrome }
shortcuts_like: chrome

A space foreach side of you.

School, work and home each get their own tabs, colors and logins. Swipe the window sideways to move between them.

spaces:
  - { name: School, profile: School, theme: forest }

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and the rest search, read and click through Valerie with your logins, in tabs you never see. When a step needs you, a little window brings it over.

valerie connect  # in a terminal

Or hideall of it.

One box finds your tabs, history, the web and your AI. Vim keys move you around. No ads, trackers or cookie banners.
